FAQ

Before you send the first message.

How much does a website cost?+

Landing pages start around RM800, while business websites and redesigns typically start around RM1,500. More complete projects require a larger budget depending on pages, content, functionality and complexity. You'll receive a project-specific quote before work begins.

What do I need to provide?+

Start with whatever you already have: business information, service details, branding, contact information, photos or existing website content. Rough material is fine.

I don't know exactly what my website needs yet. Can I still contact you?+

Yes. You don't need a formal brief first. Tell me about the business, what you currently have and what you're trying to improve. I’ll help work out an appropriate scope.

Can you redesign my existing website?+

Yes. Send me the link. I’ll review what is worth keeping and what should change before proposing the redesign scope.

How long does a project take?+

It depends on scope and content readiness. The expected timeline will be agreed before the project starts.

What happens after the website launches?+

Completed projects include a limited post-launch support period, typically around 14–30 days depending on scope, for reasonable issues related to the delivered website. Ongoing updates or maintenance can be arranged separately.
