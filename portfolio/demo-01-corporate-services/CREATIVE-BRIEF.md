> **Portfolio status: Concept Project / Demo Build**  
> Haluan Corporate Services is a fictional Malaysian business created solely to demonstrate Akizen's website strategy, design, copywriting and frontend capabilities. It is not a real Akizen client. All names, people, credentials, addresses, operating details and service descriptions in this concept are fictional unless explicitly stated otherwise. No testimonials, commercial results, revenue figures or conversion statistics should be presented as real.

---

## 0. Executive Creative Direction

### Project concept

Create a premium multi-page website for **Haluan Corporate Services**, a fictional Malaysian corporate secretarial and business compliance firm serving SMEs, founders and locally incorporated companies.

The site should make a business owner feel three things within the first minute:

1. **They understand what I need.**
2. **They look organised and credible.**
3. **I can speak to someone without being buried in jargon.**

The design should communicate **calm authority**, not corporate theatre.

Haluan should feel established enough to trust, modern enough to work efficiently, and human enough that a founder would comfortably enquire.

### Core creative idea

**Clarity for the obligations behind a growing business.**

Most compliance websites either look outdated, overly legalistic or generic. Haluan should instead frame corporate compliance as a structured business discipline: clear responsibilities, visible next steps, sensible guidance and dependable follow-through.

### Design thesis

The website should be built primarily through:

- editorial typography,
- precise grid systems,
- warm off-white space,
- restrained forest-green and mineral accents,
- high-quality Malaysian architectural/business photography,
- structured information blocks,
- elegant micro-interactions,
- thoughtful mobile hierarchy.

Avoid visual shortcuts such as glowing gradients, glassmorphism, floating 3D shapes, dashboards, chatbot bubbles, generic handshake photography or excessive icon grids.

# 4. Target Customer

## Primary audience

Malaysian business owners and directors who need dependable corporate-secretarial or statutory-administration support.

### Core customer types

**A. New founder**  
Needs to incorporate a Sdn. Bhd. and understand what happens after incorporation.

**B. Existing SME owner**  
Already has a company but feels their current secretarial support is slow, unclear or purely transactional.

**C. Growing business / finance manager**  
Needs organised support for director changes, share-related documentation, company records, resolutions and recurring statutory administration.

**D. Dormant / low-activity company owner**  
Needs help understanding routine obligations and administrative actions without unnecessary complexity.

### Audience mindset

Visitors are not browsing for entertainment. They usually arrive because:

- something needs to be filed,
- they are incorporating,
- a director/shareholder change is coming,
- their current company secretary is frustrating them,
- they are unsure what is overdue,
- they want one dependable contact to coordinate the process.

### Main anxieties

- “Am I missing something important?”
- “What exactly do I need to prepare?”
- “Will this become a complicated back-and-forth?”
- “How quickly will someone respond?”
- “Are the fees understandable?”
- “Can they explain this in normal language?”

The site should answer these anxieties through structure and tone, not exaggerated claims.

# 11. Detailed Copy Direction

## Copy strategy

Write from the visitor's immediate business situation outward.

A weak corporate-secretarial website starts with internal categories and credentials.

Haluan should instead sequence information as:

**Your situation → what the service covers → what we need → how the process works → enquiry.**

## Reading level

Professional but plain.

Prefer:

> “Tell us which company detail is changing and we will identify the information needed for the requested action.”

Instead of:

> “Our dynamic specialists provide bespoke statutory corporate governance solutions across multifaceted compliance requirements.”

## Service-page copy template

Each service page should contain:

1. **Situation-led hero**
2. **Who this service is for**
3. **What the service may include**
4. **What we need from you**
5. **How the process works**
6. **Common questions**
7. **Contextual enquiry CTA**

## Legal/compliance copy safety

Use phrases such as:

- “may include”
- “depending on the company and requested action”
- “we will confirm the applicable requirements”
- “administrative support”
- “coordination”

Avoid:

- guaranteed approval
- guaranteed compliance
- “we ensure you can never be fined”
- legal opinions
- tax optimisation claims
- definitive regulatory statements unless sourced and maintained

# 13. Visual Identity Direction

## Overall art direction

**Editorial professionalism with Malaysian architectural warmth.**

The brand should feel closer to a premium independent advisory practice or architecture studio than a generic secretarial-services provider.

### Signature visual devices

1. **Strong left-edge alignment** across large areas of the site.
2. **Fine rules and indexed section numbers** (01, 02, 03) for structure.
3. **Large serif display statements** paired with disciplined grotesk body type.
4. **Photography cropped architecturally**, prioritising space, material and gesture.
5. **Small metadata labels** used like editorial captions.

Use a wordmark rather than an elaborate symbol.

**HALUAN** in uppercase or small caps with custom letter spacing.

Possible secondary mark: a simple abstract directional line / H monogram only if extremely restrained.

Do not use compass, shield, scales-of-justice or checkmark icons.

### Layout system

Desktop:

- max content width around 1320–1440px
- 12-column grid
- generous outer margins
- sections with 120–180px vertical spacing depending on density
- intentional asymmetry

Tablet:

- 8-column grid

Mobile:

- 4-column grid
- 20–24px side padding
- 72–96px major section spacing

# 15. Typography Direction

## Recommended pairing

### Display serif

**Newsreader** (Google Fonts)

Use for:

- major headlines
- editorial pull statements
- large numerical / quote-style moments

Reason: sophisticated without looking like a luxury fashion template.

### Sans-serif

**Inter** or **Manrope**

Recommendation: **Inter** for maximum clarity and implementation reliability.

Use for:

- navigation
- body copy
- metadata
- buttons
- forms
- labels

## Scale direction

Desktop hero: 64–82px depending on viewport.

H2: 44–56px.

H3: 26–34px.

Body large: 19–21px.

Body default: 16–18px.

Metadata: 12–13px uppercase/letter-spaced selectively.

Mobile hero: 42–50px with controlled line breaks.

## Typography rules

- Avoid centre-aligning long text.
- Keep body line length around 55–72 characters.
- Use serif sparingly enough that it remains distinctive.
- Do not use six font weights.
- Let spacing and scale create hierarchy before colour.

# 18. Motion Direction

Motion should reinforce polish, not become the portfolio feature itself.

## Principles

- subtle
- functional
- low amplitude
- responsive to reduced-motion settings

## Recommended motion

### Hero entrance

Headline and supporting copy fade/translate upward 12–20px with slight stagger.

Image reveals via a restrained vertical mask.

Total perceived duration under ~800ms.

### Service rows

On desktop hover:

- arrow moves 4–6px
- divider / accent line expands
- optional small image crop fades into the right side

No 3D tilt.

### Section transitions

Light opacity / translate-on-enter for major blocks.

Do not animate every paragraph.

### Numbers / indexed labels

No count-up statistics because there are no real statistics to prove.

### Navigation

Compact header transition after scroll; 180–250ms.

### FAQ

Smooth height expansion, accessible state change.

### Reduced motion

All decorative motion should collapse to instant state changes under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
