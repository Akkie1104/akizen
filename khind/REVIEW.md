# KHIND customer-page draft

Prepared 27 September 2026 for Akizen's `/khind/` route. Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript; no build step. Preview from the repository root with a local HTTP server.

## Included

- Bahasa Melayu customer catalogue: 13 models, 10 bundle choices, Basic/Premium where available, and outright prices.
- Category filters, model search, native accessible product dialog and plan selector.
- First-six-month rates, later rates, contract duration, full rental total and RM1 processing fee.
- Malaysia-time expiry of Q3 and outright offers; DHP90 October–December offer activates only during its dates.
- Product-specific WhatsApp enquiries to **0174201247 / 60174201247**, explicitly supplied by the owner.
- Plain-language workflow, approval caveat, payment and document boundaries, warranty and termination disclosures.

## Review before publication

This remains a local draft, marked `noindex,nofollow`, with a visible review banner. The homepage, sitemap and production hosting were not changed.

The researched agent agreement clause 7.1 requires prior KHIND written consent for independently created advertising. Obtain that consent and confirm the final copy before publishing. Then remove the draft banner and change indexing metadata intentionally.

Confirm stock, the final first-payment amount, promotional eligibility and any fee exclusions. No promotional stacking is assumed. CD12D has no confirmed price; WD1438's RM1,899 plan is labelled as an unconfirmed separate Sabah offer with one-year warranty. No exact delivery date, guaranteed approval or Shariah certification is claimed.

Official public product photographs are referenced directly from KHIND's CDN. WD1438 and CD12D have model-name placeholders because no matching public image was verified. Bundle cards use a text treatment. ACSON photographs represent the Kool series, not a verified visual distinction between horsepower variants. External image availability depends on KHIND's hosting.

Internal research and PDFs remain outside this repository, in the sibling `output/khind` folder. Do not publish them. Source basis: agent POS and dated campaign memos recorded in `khind-catalogue-and-workflow-2026-09-27.md`; public product-page links are retained in `catalogue.js`, and the official FAQ is linked in the page.
