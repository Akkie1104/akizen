# KHIND storefront visual direction

Audience: Malaysian households comparing appliance needs and payment commitments.
Surface mode: Persuade, with an accessible product-selection workflow.
Scope: deeper visual overhaul of /khind/; preserve catalogue truth, URLs, main navigation, payment calculations, disclosures and enquiry destinations.

The first pass used yellow/charcoal, platform typography, a restrained split hero, outlined category tiles and three-column products. Retire its generic display font, decorative eyebrows, faux brand dot, excessive lead-in text and timid appliance composition.

Direction: a warm appliance editorial with Manrope typography, an expansive KHIND-yellow hero, oversized real appliance imagery, photographic category selectors, open product rows, and a concise advisor close. White/graphite page themes follow system preference with an explicit theme toggle. Yellow remains the sole accent.

Taste dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 7, MOTION_INTENSITY 3, VISUAL_DENSITY 4. Appliances and payment disclosures need focus; no perpetual animations or scroll hijacking. One short hero reveal; native interaction feedback elsewhere.

Native HTML/CSS/JavaScript remains the stack. Self-host Manrope (SIL OFL); vendor Tabler 3.35.0 icons (MIT). Product photography comes from existing official catalogue URLs, never competitor photography. Overlay labels must stay off imagery; offers stay with prices.

Layer scale: base 0, local decoration 1, sticky navigation 10, skip-link 50; native dialog uses the browser top layer.

Source skills: Impeccable 9d715cc4f5564a990ca8345abfdd5df6dc9b41c8 and Taste ce26fc25c0e5e8cab638f883de62d9a86ee5e45b. Their contextual rules inform the design; existing commercial disclosures and meaningful repeated enquiry actions take precedence over blanket copy/CTA limits.
