# Shared catalogue backdrop

The original `studio-sage.png` backdrop was generated on 27 September 2026, but it has been retired from the customer page after the 28 September UI/UX audit because the decorative PNG was about 1.9 MB. The hero and catalogue panels now recreate the same pale-sage studio direction with lightweight CSS gradients, so no decorative background image is downloaded. Official product photographs remain unchanged.

## Original generation prompt (archived for reference)

Create a photorealistic empty product photography studio backdrop for a Malaysian home-appliance catalogue website. Landscape 3:2 composition. A softly textured pale sage green plaster wall meets a warm ivory matte floor at the lower quarter of the image, subtle rounded architectural corner at far right, soft daylight from upper left with a very faint broad window shadow on the far left wall, restrained premium domestic editorial aesthetic, low contrast, evenly illuminated center and generous empty space for compositing real appliance photographs. Palette sage #e5e9de, ivory #f7f5ef, muted olive shadows. Camera straight-on, natural perspective, no dramatic pedestal. Absolutely no appliances, no products, no furniture, no plants, no people, no text, no logos, no watermark. The background must be subtle enough to repeat across a catalogue without competing with product photos.
